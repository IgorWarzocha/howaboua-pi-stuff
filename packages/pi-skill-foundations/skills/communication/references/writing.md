Write prose with a real job, context, and point of view. Remove generic model polish without forcing every writer into the same dry, quirky, or aggressively casual voice.

## Preserve the substance and author

Know what the reader should understand, believe, feel, or do. Keep facts, supplied opinions, assumptions, uncertainty, and creative invention distinct. Cleaner prose must not imply stronger evidence or confidence.

For an existing draft, preserve the author's stance, vocabulary, humour, restraint, regional conventions, and deliberate oddness within the core construction rules. For a new draft, derive voice from the writer, audience, purpose, venue, and relevant examples. Never substitute a generic "human" persona.

Respect the genre. An email, essay, technical guide, product page, personal note, UI label, and story should not share one house voice. Ground specificity in supplied context or verified facts unless the task is explicitly creative.

## Make the point visible

Lead with the point, event, question, or concrete observation. Give each paragraph distinct work and use only as much structure as the material needs. A short thought does not need a framework. A long sentence is fine when its ideas belong together.

Keep quirks that carry the author's voice. If a memorable phrase starts repeating, preserve its strongest use and write the others plainly. Read for cadence when rhythm matters, but stop polishing before voice disappears.

## Diagnose the delta

The core skill owns ordinary AI-slop removal. A full prose diagnosis should go one layer deeper and identify what produced the synthetic effect: a missing point, unsupported claim, wrong genre, flattened authorial voice, mechanical structure, or context the draft never had. Fix that cause rather than hunting forbidden words.

Treat suspicious phrases as evidence, not convictions. Preserve genuine smoothness, polish, warmth, enthusiasm, and necessary repetition. Do not make prose "human" by injecting fragments, slang, profanity, fake jokes, or deliberate roughness.

Genre changes the diagnosis. Technical writing may need exact terminology, warnings, and qualifications. Essays need argument and deliberate transitions. Emails need the real reason or question. Marketing must earn desire through behaviour or evidence. Social writing should preserve natural timing. UI copy should be brief in context. Creative writing may invent and stylize within the requested form.

When asked to write or rewrite, lead with the finished prose. When asked for diagnosis, explain the important pattern and quote only what helps. Use comparisons only when they make several edits easier to judge.

Then stop. Repeated polishing can remove the voice this reference exists to protect.
