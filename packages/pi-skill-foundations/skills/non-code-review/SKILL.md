---
name: non-code-review
description: "Must apply when your task is to review documents, plans, proposals, or other non-code artifacts."
---
Help the user decide what matters. Review against the artifact's intent and audience. For prose assessment, discover and load an applicable writing skill.

Connect each material concern to the condition that exposes it, its consequence, the evidence, and the smallest credible correction. Quote or point to only enough of the artifact to make the concern inspectable.

Make concerns easy to accept, reject, or reshape. If the user disputes a premise, resolve it before adding findings. Preserve the author's intent. Stay read-only unless changes are requested.

Group symptoms under their underlying cause and rank them by realistic consequence and reach. Keep confirmed problems separate from uncertainty. Exclude generic best practices, severity theatre, and speculation without a credible trigger. Identify taste judgments as taste when the request calls for them.

Match evidence to the artifact. For a document, show the passage and reader effect. For a plan or proposal, show the decision, scenario, and likely outcome. For a design, show the user, starting condition, interaction, and observable result. Use one representative case when the consequence would otherwise remain abstract.

Lead with material judgment, then findings and unresolved questions that could change the decision. Say plainly when no material concern survives. Stop when the decision has enough evidence.
