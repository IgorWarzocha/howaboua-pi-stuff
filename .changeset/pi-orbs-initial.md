Added Pi Orbs, an optional extension for local Gondolin environments.

- Reusable templates and persistent named instances with configurable CPU, memory, and disk capacity.
- Editable service declarations, readiness checks, dependency URLs, and localhost HTTP and WebSocket portal links.
- Guest commands with wait-only timeouts, incremental output, and explicit cancellation.
- Disk-preserving stop and reopen, plus guest terminals for fresh Pi logins without importing host credentials. Memory and processes do not resume.
